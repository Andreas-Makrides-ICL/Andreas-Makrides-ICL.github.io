  • Under review

    Learning to Fix: Optimisation-Aware Machine Learning for Accelerated Unit Commitment

    B. Fritz, A. Makrides, M. Fetanat, P. Pinson. Submitted to IEEE Transactions on Power Systems.

    The method that won 1st place in the 2025 EPRI AI-ccelerating Unit Commitment competition. Generator-specific confidence thresholds decide which binary decisions to fix, giving a 20.8× average speed-up over Gurobi at a 0.48% mean optimality gap.

    Ancillary Services in High-Renewable Power Systems: Comparing Market Design, Emerging Trends, and Future Challenges

    A. Makrides, A. Churkin, F. Teng, P. Pinson. arXiv preprint.

    A comparison of ancillary-service market design in Great Britain, Germany, Denmark, Italy, California, Texas and Australia, with a product-level classification of services and the trends and challenges as renewables grow.

    Quantifying the Impact of Missing Risk Markets on Investment in Decarbonized Power Systems with Long-Duration Energy Storage

    A. C. Makrides, A. Suski, E. Spyrou. Submitted to Sustainable Energy, Grids and Networks.

    How incomplete markets for trading risk, together with risk aversion, reduce investment in long-duration energy storage, studied with equilibrium models of a GB test system.
